## What is Caprae's Mission?

Caprae Capital's mission is to prove that private equity value creation doesn't have to come
from financial engineering — it comes from operators actually improving the businesses they
acquire. Their SaaS and MaaS (M&A-as-a-Service) models treat an acquisition as the *start*
of a seven-year value-creation journey, not the finish line, and a core part of that journey
is helping portfolio companies adopt practical AI to improve decision-making, streamline
operations, and grow. In short: they're trying to change what "private equity" means in
practice — from spreadsheet arbitrage to hands-on, technology-driven company building.

## How is Caprae Changing the ETA Space and Broader PE?

Traditional ETA (Entrepreneurship Through Acquisition) and PE largely optimize the
transaction — find a business, finance it, extract value through cost-cutting or leverage,
exit. Caprae is positioning itself differently by treating the *post-acquisition* period as
where most of the value is actually created, and by productizing that support (SaaS/MaaS)
rather than leaving it to ad hoc consulting. Embedding AI-readiness directly into how they
evaluate and support portfolio companies — and using an unconventional, execution-focused
interview process like this handbook to find people who fit that operator mindset — is a
bet that technology-literate, hands-on operators outperform financial engineering over a
multi-year hold period.
